> **IMPORTANT NOTICE — PLEASE READ BEFORE SIGNING**
> This Agreement limits the scope of Hayes Yacht Company's services, excludes continuous monitoring, permits vessel movement only under a separate signed Vessel Delivery Authorization, allocates certain risks to Client, and contains limitations of liability and damages. Client should review the entire Agreement and may wish to consult legal counsel before signing.

This Vessel-Watch & Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of the Effective Date shown in Exhibit A by and between Hayes Charters LLC, doing business as Hayes Yacht Company ("Company"), and the vessel owner or authorized contracting party identified in Exhibit A ("Client"). Company and Client may each be a "Party" and together the "Parties."

## 1. Agreement Documents and Order of Control

**1.1** This Agreement consists of this document and the completed Exhibit A (Service Plan). The Client Onboarding Questionnaire may be used to collect contact, vessel, safety, access, and operational information, but is not itself a contract document, does not grant service authority, and does not expand or amend this Agreement. Any later change to services, permissions, or charges must be in a writing accepted by both Parties, including a signed or electronically accepted amendment. Company's internal standard operating procedures are not part of this Agreement.

**1.2** If documents conflict, this Agreement controls, followed by the then-current Exhibit A and any later mutually accepted written amendment. The Questionnaire and operational setup records may provide facts, instructions, preferences, or restrictions but cannot override the Agreement or add an authorized activity. Company's internal standard operating procedures are not part of this Agreement.

## 2. Scope of Vessel-Watch Services

**2.1** Company will perform the scheduled visual, non-destructive inspection and reporting services selected in Exhibit A. Observations will be documented through Boat Binder or another electronic reporting method designated by Company.

**2.2** A report describes conditions reasonably visible and accessible at the date and time of the visit. Company does not promise that every developing problem will be visible, discoverable, or reported during a scheduled inspection.

**2.3** Only the checks selected and authorized in Exhibit A are included. Questionnaire answers and operational onboarding records may restrict how or whether a selected check is performed, but do not authorize a check. Any repair, maintenance, cleaning, emergency work, or additional visit is outside the recurring service unless separately authorized in a writing accepted by both Parties. Vendor coordination is included only to the extent stated in Exhibit A.

**2.4 Storm checks.** If Exhibit A includes storm checks, Company will make one additional visit after a National Weather Service wind advisory, high wind warning, gale warning, or storm warning that covers the vessel's location, within the time stated in Exhibit A after the warning ends and conditions are safe. Storm checks are otherwise subject to Sections 4.3 and 5.5.

**2.5 Boat Binder access.** While this Agreement is in effect, Client will have access to the Boat Binder app for the covered vessel at no additional charge. Client's use of Boat Binder is also subject to Boat Binder's terms of use.

## 3. Expressly Excluded Services

**3.1** Company does not provide a marine survey, appraisal, structural inspection, engineering analysis, mechanical diagnosis, electrical certification, environmental assessment, security service, watchman service, salvage service, towing service, or continuous real-time monitoring.

**3.2** Except during a delivery authorized under Section 6A, Company will not engage a propulsion transmission or gear, navigate, reposition, relocate, fuel, tow, haul, launch, deliver, or otherwise operate or move the vessel under this Agreement. A dockside engine-start check performed under Section 6 does not authorize vessel movement.

**3.3** Company does not disassemble equipment, enter unsafe or confined spaces, disturb sealed systems, operate equipment identified as restricted, or test hidden components. Company may decline any action that it reasonably considers unsafe, outside the agreed scope, or inconsistent with manufacturer instructions.

## 4. Scheduled and Intermittent Nature of Services

**4.1** Visits occur only at the frequency and general schedule listed in Exhibit A, plus any storm checks Exhibit A includes. Company does not maintain a continuous presence and has no duty to observe or respond to conditions arising between visits.

**4.2** The vessel's condition may change at any time because of weather, tides or water levels, marina operations, shore-power interruptions, battery depletion, pump or equipment failure, water intrusion, chafing, theft, vandalism, fire, collision, pollution, pests, or other events. Except to the extent directly caused by Company's breach of this Agreement, Company is not responsible for conditions that begin, develop, worsen, or cause loss between scheduled visits.

**4.3** Inspection dates and times are approximate unless Exhibit A expressly states otherwise. Company may reschedule or defer a visit because of weather, darkness, unsafe access, marina restrictions, emergencies, illness, or other circumstances beyond Company's reasonable control.

## 5. Access, Boarding, and Client Responsibilities

**5.1** Client authorizes Company and its authorized personnel to enter the marina and board the vessel solely to perform services expressly authorized in Exhibit A. Client will provide lawful and functional access, current contact information, and any required marina approval.

**5.2** Client will maintain reasonably safe boarding points, decks, steps, ladders, railings, and walking surfaces and will disclose known hazards, cameras, alarms, animals, sensitive finishes, restricted compartments, and equipment that must not be operated.

**5.3** Client will provide accurate vessel, insurance, safety, access, and emergency information and promptly update it. Before a particular service activity, Client must provide the equipment-specific baseline, instructions, and photographs reasonably required for that activity. Company may rely on information and operational restrictions supplied by Client until Client provides a written update. Questionnaire answers do not independently authorize an activity.

**5.4** Actual access codes and passwords must be delivered through a secure method approved by Company and should not be placed in ordinary email or on the printed Onboarding Questionnaire.

**5.5** If access is unavailable or the vessel is unsafe to board, Company may document a safety or access deferral, notify Client, and charge the scheduled visit if personnel traveled to the vessel and were unable to perform because of circumstances within Client's control.

## 6. Authorized Routine System Checks

**6.1** Company may open designated access panels, read visible gauges and status displays, photograph systems, and briefly operate only those switches, breakers, or manual overrides specifically selected and authorized in Exhibit A. Client's operational instructions may narrow or prohibit a check; they may not add permission. If an instruction is missing, ambiguous, or conflicts with Exhibit A, Company may defer the activity until the Parties clarify or amend Exhibit A in writing.

**6.2** Company will use reasonable care and return accessed equipment, selectors, panels, locks, alarms, and climate settings to the documented baseline. Client acknowledges that marine equipment may fail without warning because of age, corrosion, installation, latent defects, wear, or loss of power.

**6.3** Company is not responsible for a component failure that occurs during a reasonable authorized check unless the failure was directly caused by Company's negligent or intentionally improper operation of that component.

**6.4** If expressly selected in Exhibit A, Client authorizes Company to start and briefly run only the identified propulsion engine(s) and/or generator(s), solely as a dockside operational check. The vessel must remain securely moored, each transmission must remain in neutral, and Company will not engage gear or attempt to place an engine under propulsion load. Company may observe startup, cooling-water flow, visible gauges, alarms, smoke, vibration, leakage, and shutdown, but this check is not a mechanical inspection, diagnosis, repair, sea trial, or warranty of engine or generator condition.

**6.5** Company will follow the run-time limits and written startup or shutdown instructions in Exhibit A and may decline or stop a check if access, ventilation, cooling-water flow, fuel or fluid level, weather, marina rules, vessel condition, or any other circumstance appears unsafe. Client is responsible for identifying required pre-start procedures, known defects, restricted engines or generators, and manufacturer-specific instructions before the applicable service begins.

## 6A. Vessel Deliveries (Optional Service)

**6A.1** If Client signs a separate Vessel Delivery Authorization in the form of Exhibit B (including by email acceptance of a completed Exhibit B), Company's USCG-licensed captain will operate the vessel solely to deliver it between the origin and destination stated there, by the stated route, within the stated date window. Each delivery, or each round trip, requires its own authorization. Exhibit B is outside the initial subscription enrollment workflow and does not grant authority unless separately completed and accepted for that delivery.

**6A.2** Company's captain alone decides whether weather, tide, current, visibility, traffic, lock conditions, and the vessel's condition permit a safe delivery, and may delay, reroute, shorten, or cancel a delivery for safety. Client pays only for time actually spent when a delivery is cancelled for safety.

**6A.3** Client represents that the vessel is seaworthy, carries the safety equipment required by law, and has working navigation lights, steering, propulsion, and VHF radio, and that Client's hull insurance permits operation by a hired licensed captain. Client will provide written confirmation from its insurer on request.

**6A.4** Company may require a second crew member, including for any vessel over forty (40) feet transiting the Hiram M. Chittenden (Ballard) Locks, and may decline any delivery it considers unsafe or that would exceed the per-vessel limit of Company's marine liability insurance stated in Exhibit B.

**6A.5** Client will pay the delivery fees stated in Exhibit B and will pay or reimburse fuel, lock, dockage, pump-out, yard, and haul-out charges, and the cost of any required second crew member.

**6A.6** Company has care of the vessel during a delivery only from the time the vessel leaves its origin berth until it is secured at the destination and the destination facility or Client has been notified. For loss to the vessel during that period, the limit in Section 13.1 is replaced by the amount recoverable for that event under Company's marine liability insurance; Section 13.2 continues to apply. Section 17.2 otherwise applies.

## 7. Reports, Photographs, and Notifications

**7.1** Company will deliver routine reports to the recipients listed in Exhibit A. Client is responsible for reviewing reports and arranging any recommended follow-up, service, repair, or professional evaluation.

**7.2** Client authorizes Company to photograph the vessel, its systems, and observed conditions for inspection records, quality control, claim documentation, and service administration. Company will not use identifiable Client or vessel images for public marketing without separate permission.

**7.3** Company will use the routine and urgent notification methods selected in Exhibit A. Delivery to the contact information supplied by Client satisfies Company's reporting obligation even if Client does not promptly open, read, or respond to the communication.

## 8. Urgent Conditions and Emergency Response

**8.1** If Company observes a condition that reasonably appears urgent, Company will make reasonable efforts to contact Client and the designated emergency contacts using the order and methods in Exhibit A.

**8.2** When Client cannot be reached, Company may notify marina personnel, emergency services, the United States Coast Guard, or a designated vendor when Company reasonably believes notice is appropriate. Company has no duty to place personnel at risk or to take action beyond the authority selected in Exhibit A.

**8.3** Company may incur third-party charges only up to the emergency spending limit stated in Exhibit A, unless Client separately approves a higher amount. A blank spending limit means zero dollars. Client remains responsible for authorized third-party charges, which may be billed with an administrative coordination fee if listed in Exhibit A.

**8.4** Emergency authority does not authorize Company to navigate, reposition, relocate, tow, salvage, haul, launch, fuel, or otherwise move or operate the vessel. Any such service requires a Vessel Delivery Authorization under Section 6A or a separate written agreement with an appropriately insured provider.

**8.5** Company does not guarantee that it or any vendor will be available or able to prevent, contain, or reduce damage during an emergency.

## 9. Fees, Invoices, and Third-Party Charges

By signing this Agreement and completing the payment authorization presented separately during enrollment, Client authorizes Company and its payment processor, Stripe, to charge the payment method Client provides for the exact recurring subscription amount, cadence, applicable disclosed taxes, and separately authorized charges stated in Exhibit A. Monthly or annual recurring charges renew on each corresponding billing anniversary of the initial payment date; billing is not aligned to the first day of a calendar month. The annual prepayment discount is ten percent (10%) off the then-applicable equivalent monthly recurring fees for the twelve-month prepaid period. Exhibit A must state the actual amount charged, billing cadence, initial charge, renewal date basis, and any discount. No amount may be changed through the Questionnaire or an operational record.

**9.2 Additional charges.** Client will reimburse approved third-party costs, marina charges, parking, materials, taxes, and extraordinary expenses. Company will not mark up a third-party charge unless an administrative or coordination fee is disclosed in Exhibit A or separately approved. This Agreement does not authorize an unspecified automatic charge.

## 10. Term, Suspension, and Termination

**10.1 Term and renewal.** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for the initial subscription period selected in Exhibit A, if any, and thereafter renews for successive periods matching the selected monthly or annual billing cadence unless cancelled or terminated under this Section. A recurring billing anniversary is measured from the date of the first subscription payment, not the first day of a calendar month. Client may cancel future renewal at any time by written notice, including email or a Boat Binder message, received before the next renewal charge is processed. Cancellation stops future renewal charges but does not itself shorten a prepaid service period or waive amounts already due. Service continues through the paid service period unless this Agreement is earlier terminated or suspended under its terms. Company may terminate without cause on thirty (30) days' written notice.

**10.2** Company may suspend or terminate immediately for unsafe conditions, unlawful instructions, loss of access, material misrepresentation, abusive conduct, nonpayment, insurance concerns, or a material breach by Client. Where appropriate and required by law, Company will provide notice and a reasonable opportunity to cure before suspension or termination.

**10.3** Upon termination, Client will pay for services performed and authorized costs incurred through the termination date. Company will return Company-held physical keys or access devices as reasonably directed after all undisputed amounts are paid. Termination and cancellation do not authorize an automatic refund outside Section 10.4.

**10.4** If Client prepaid recurring fees for a period after the termination date, Company will refund the prepaid amount less the standard monthly rate, without any prepayment discount, for each month of service begun before termination. The one-time onboarding fee is not refundable once the baseline visit has been performed.

**10.5** Client's no-charge Boat Binder access under Section 2.5 ends thirty (30) days after termination. During that period Client may export available records or continue under Boat Binder's then-current subscription terms. **[ATTORNEY REVIEW: consider access to executed agreement and signature evidence beyond this period.]**

## 11. Client Insurance and Vessel Eligibility

**11.1** Client will maintain current hull and machinery insurance and protection and indemnity or other appropriate marine liability coverage for the vessel and will provide proof upon request.

Client will notify Company before any change in ownership, location, value, use, insurance, or condition that could materially affect the service risk.

## 12. No Warranties or Guarantees

**12.1** Company makes no representation or warranty, express or implied, regarding the vessel's seaworthiness, condition, merchantability, fitness for use, security, regulatory compliance, or future performance.

**12.2** A completed report is not a guarantee that the vessel is dry, secure, safe, mechanically sound, insured, or free from hidden defects. Client retains ultimate responsibility for vessel maintenance, repair, safety, compliance, and insurance.

## 13. Limitation of Liability and Damages

**13.1** To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Company's total aggregate liability for all claims arising from one event or a series of related events will not exceed the greater of (a) the fees actually paid to Company for recurring services during the three (3) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim or (b) five hundred dollars ($500.00).

**13.2** To the fullest extent permitted by law, neither Company nor its owners, employees, or authorized agents will be liable for indirect, incidental, special, exemplary, punitive, or consequential damages, loss of use, loss of charter revenue, diminution in value, or loss of profits, even if advised that such damages were possible.

**13.3** The limitations in this Section apply to claims in contract, tort, negligence, strict liability, statute, or any other theory, but do not apply to liability that applicable law does not permit the Parties to limit or exclude, or to Company's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

**13.4** Client acknowledges that the service fees reflect this allocation of risk and that broader monitoring, diagnostic, security, emergency-response, or liability obligations would require different services, pricing, and insurance.

## 14. Indemnification

**14.1** To the fullest extent permitted by law, Client will indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Company and its owners, employees, and authorized agents from third-party claims, liabilities, penalties, cleanup costs, property damage, and reasonable attorneys' fees to the extent arising from (a) the vessel's condition; (b) Client's breach, instructions, or misrepresentation; (c) an undisclosed hazard; or (d) fuel, oil, sewage, or other pollution not caused by Company.

**14.2** Client's obligations do not apply to the extent a final determination attributes the claim to Company's negligence, gross negligence, or willful misconduct. Each Party remains responsible for loss to the extent caused by that Party.

## 15. Confidentiality and Records

**15.1** Company will use reasonable measures to protect nonpublic Client information, access information, reports, and photographs and may disclose them to personnel, insurers, professional advisers, vendors, marina personnel, or authorities as reasonably necessary to perform services, respond to an incident, comply with law, or address a claim.

**15.2** Company may retain service records for its operational, insurance, tax, and legal needs. Client may download or request copies of available reports while the account remains active and during the period stated in Section 10.5.

## 16. Governing Law and Disputes

**16.1** The Parties will first attempt in good faith to resolve a dispute through direct discussion. This requirement does not prevent either Party from seeking urgent injunctive or protective relief.

**16.2** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington, without regard to conflict-of-law rules, subject to any federal maritime law that must apply. Exclusive venue will lie in the state or federal courts located in King County, Washington, and each Party consents to personal jurisdiction there.

**16.3** In an action to enforce this Agreement, the substantially prevailing Party may recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs to the extent permitted by law.
