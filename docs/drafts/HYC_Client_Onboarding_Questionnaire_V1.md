## Collected during operational onboarding (not required to sign)

Gather through HYC's secure operational setup after contract execution. Complete the applicable items before the related inspection or equipment action. Pending setup means the activity is deferred; it does not suspend unrelated visual checks unless safety/access requires it.

- Actual access provisioning through an approved secure method; never ordinary questionnaire/email.
- Vessel-specific do-not-operate list, access-panel map, hazards, boarding constraints, marina procedures, and emergency access method.
- Baseline photographs, panel labels, gauges, status displays, selectors, alarms, and settings relevant to agreed checks.
- Battery/charger/inverter baseline readings and safe display interpretation.
- Bilge layout, normal water level, pump status, alarm state, and instructions; manual pump override procedure (SOP-001 limits dry override tests to no more than three seconds).
- Climate, HVAC, heater and dehumidifier baseline/status; manufacturer-specific operation and freezing-weather instructions.
- Plumbing/sanitation/refrigeration/pest system baseline and areas not to open or test.
- For any separately authorized engine/generator run: exact unit identity, owner-supplied start/stop instructions, ventilation/blower requirements, seacock and cooling-water checks, fuel/fluid prerequisites, maximum runtime, neutral/moored limits, observed baseline, defects and conditions requiring cancellation.
- Report template, baseline/departure photo set, Boat Binder setup, and assigned HYC internal checklist.
