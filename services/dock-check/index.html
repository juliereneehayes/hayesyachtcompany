Dock Check

A consistent, documented view of your boat from the dock—without an aboard visit.

A straightforward watch

Who Dock Check is for

Dock Check is for owners who want scheduled eyes on their boat from the dock. It offers a simple way to stay connected to a boat between owner visits without choosing an aboard-check plan.

What’s included

  • A dockside visual check at the frequency you select
  • Documented photo updates through Boat Binder

Photo documentation & updates

Each scheduled visit includes photo documentation managed through Boat Binder, giving you a record of the visit and what HYC observed from the dock.

Important considerations

Dock Check stays dockside. It does not include going aboard, operating equipment, or checking aboard systems. Maintenance, repairs, and diagnostic work are not included.
