Aboard care plan

Dock Watch

A full aboard check at the monthly, every-other-week, or weekly frequency that fits your boat.

More visibility aboard

Who Dock Watch is for

Dock Watch is for owners of 30–60 ft boats who want scheduled aboard observations and a documented update between their own visits. Choose the frequency that matches how often you want HYC aboard.

What’s included

  • A full aboard check at your selected frequency
  • Documented photo updates through Boat Binder

Photo documentation & updates

Each scheduled visit includes photo documentation managed through Boat Binder, creating a clear record of the visit and HYC’s observations.

Important considerations

Dock Watch is an observation and reporting plan. A scheduled engine run is not included; Dock Watch Plus adds one monthly engine run. Maintenance, repairs, and diagnostic work are separate from the plan.
